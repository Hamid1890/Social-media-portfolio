# Short-Form Video Content Strategy

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates my ability to plan short-form video content for social media platforms such as TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

## Campaign Objective

The objective is to create engaging short-form videos that increase reach, audience engagement, brand awareness, and follower growth.

## Target Audience

Young adults aged 18–30 who actively use TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube.

## Platforms

- TikTok
- Instagram Reels
- YouTube Shorts

## Content Strategy

The strategy focuses on:

- Short and engaging videos
- Strong hooks
- Trending topics
- Educational content
- Entertainment
- Clear calls-to-action
- Consistent posting

## Posting Strategy

- Post consistently
- Test different posting times
- Use relevant hashtags
- Respond to comments
- Analyze performance
- Repurpose successful content

## Performance Metrics

I would track:

- Views
- Reach
- Watch time
- Engagement rate
- Likes
- Comments
- Shares
- Saves
- Followers gained
