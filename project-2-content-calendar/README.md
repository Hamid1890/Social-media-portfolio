# 30-Day Social Media Content Calendar

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates my ability to plan and organize social media content consistently over a 30-day period.

## Campaign Goal

The goal is to maintain consistent posting, increase audience engagement, build brand awareness, and attract new followers.

## Target Audience

Young adults aged 18–30 who are interested in digital marketing, lifestyle, and online content.

## Platforms

- Instagram
- TikTok
- Facebook

## Content Pillars

- Educational
- Entertaining
- Promotional
- Engagement
- Behind the Scenes
